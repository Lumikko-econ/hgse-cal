How often the feeds update, and how to remove one

Three times on a working day — early morning, 13.00 and 18.00 — once on Saturday morning, and not on Sunday. Your calendar application decides when it fetches them; the feeds ask to be re-read every six hours.

To remove one: in Outlook, right-click it in the calendar list and choose Remove; in Apple Calendar, right-click and choose Delete; in Google Calendar, open Settings and choose Unsubscribe.
