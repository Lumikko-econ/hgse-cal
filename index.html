Add a series to your calendar

Subscribe once and your calendar is kept up to date automatically, usually within hours: new talks, changed times and rooms, and cancellations.

Each series you add is a calendar of its own, so your calendar app can show each one in its own colour.

Which button? With an Aalto or University of Helsinki account, Outlook, on a computer; the calendar then shows on your phone too. With Apple Calendar on an iPhone or a Mac, Apple. With Google Calendar, Google, on a computer.

Outlook button not working?

On a phone, Outlook cannot add these calendars, in the app or in the browser. Open this page on a computer and press Outlook there; the calendar then shows on your phone too.

On a computer, add the calendar by hand instead; this way does not use the button. Press Copy beside the calendar you want, open Outlook on the web, choose Add calendar, then Subscribe from web, paste the address, and choose Import.

Or open the button’s link in a private window and sign in with your university account. If that works, your browser is holding an old Microsoft sign-in: sign out of Outlook on the web, or clear cookies and site data for the Microsoft sites.

Colours, and calendars side by side

A seminar run jointly by two series, or a practice job talk, is in the calendar of each series it belongs to, so taking both shows it twice, once in each colour.

To change a calendar’s colour: in Outlook, right-click it and choose Color; in Google Calendar, click the three dots next to it and pick a colour; in Apple Calendar, you pick one as you subscribe, and can change it later by Control-clicking the calendar on a Mac, or tapping the info button next to it on an iPhone.

Outlook showing your calendars side by side instead of in one view? In Outlook on the web and the new Outlook, switch off Split view. In classic Outlook for Windows, click the arrow on each calendar’s tab (View in Overlay Mode). In Outlook for Mac, choose View, then Overlay, in Day, Work Week or Week view.

How quickly changes reach your calendar

Usually within an hour of a change on the Helsinki GSE events page, the feeds have it. Your calendar app then picks it up when it next checks: Outlook every few hours, so a change can take several hours, occasionally a day, to show.

How to remove a calendar

In Outlook, right-click it in the calendar list and choose Remove (Delete Calendar in classic Outlook for Windows); in Apple Calendar, right-click it and choose Unsubscribe; in Google Calendar, open its Settings and choose Unsubscribe.
